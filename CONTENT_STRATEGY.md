## 📊 BLOG POST TEMPLATES

### Template 1: Listicle (High Shareability)
**Structure:**
1. Hook intro (2-3 sentences)
2. Brief context paragraph
3. #1 Item with 150-200 word explanation
4. #2 Item...
5. (Continue for all items)
6. Conclusion with CTA

**Optimal Length:** 1,500-2,000 words  
**Images:** One per item + featured image

### Template 2: Deep Dive Analysis (SEO)
**Structure:**
1. Table of contents
2. Introduction with keyword
3. Section 1: Background/History
4. Section 2: Core Analysis
5. Section 3: Interpretations
6. Section 4: Modern Applications
7. Section 5: Common Questions (FAQ)
8. Conclusion with strong CTA

**Optimal Length:** 2,500-3,500 words  
**Images:** 5-7 custom graphics

### Template 3: Personal Story (Engagement)
**Structure:**
1. Hook: Start with the moment
2. Background: How you got here
3. The Challenge/Conflict
4. The Insight/Turning Point
5. The Outcome/Lesson
6. How Readers Can Apply It
7. CTA: Share your story/take quiz

**Optimal Length:** 1,200-1,800 words  
**Images:** 2-3 relevant visuals

## 🎨 CONTENT CREATION WORKFLOW

### Step 1: Research (30 min)
- Google the topic
- Check what's ranking
- Find gaps in existing content
- List 3 unique angles

### Step 2: Outline (15 min)
- H2 and H3 headings
- Key points under each
- Note where to add images/CTA

### Step 3: Write (60-90 min)
- First draft fast, edit later
- Write like you talk
- Use examples and stories
- Add personality

### Step 4: Optimize (30 min)
- Add keyword naturally
- Meta description (155 chars)
- Alt text for images
- Internal links (3-5)
- External links (2-3 authority sites)

### Step 5: Format (15 min)
- Break up long paragraphs
- Add bullet points
- Bold key phrases
- Insert images
- Add CTAs (mid-article + end)

### Step 6: Publish & Promote (30 min)
- Upload to site
- Share on social media
- Email to list (if relevant)
- Submit to relevant communities
- Schedule reposts

**Total Time Per Post:** 3-4 hours

## 🔗 INTERNAL LINKING STRATEGY

### Hub & Spoke Model

**Hub Page:** Main topic page (e.g., "Dragon Mythology Guide")  
**Spoke Pages:** Related posts linking back to hub

Example:
- Hub: "Complete Guide to Dragon Mythology"
  - Spoke: "5 Types of Dragons"
  - Spoke: "Dragon Symbolism in Religion"
  - Spoke: "Famous Dragons in History"
  - Spoke: "Dragon Meditation Techniques"

### Link From Every Post
- Link to homepage (in nav)
- Link to 2-3 related blog posts
- Link to pricing page or quiz (CTA)
- Link to most popular post
- Link to newest post

## 🎯 CONVERSION OPTIMIZATION FOR BLOG

### Strategic CTA Placement

**Top of Post** (after intro):
- Quiz banner: "Discover your dragon type in 2 minutes"

**Middle of Post** (after section 2-3):
- Email opt-in: "Get the Dragon Meditation Guide free"
- CTA box: "Read 3 free chapters from Genesis"

**End of Post** (before comments):
- Pricing link: "Upgrade to read the full library"
- Quiz link: "What dragon resonates with you?"

**Sidebar** (if using):
- Email form (always visible)
- Most popular posts
- Quiz CTA

### A/B Test These Elements
- CTA button color (gold vs red vs green)
- CTA text ("Start Free" vs "Get Access" vs "Join Now")
- CTA placement (top vs middle vs both)
- Image vs no image in CTA box
- Short form vs long form email opt-in
