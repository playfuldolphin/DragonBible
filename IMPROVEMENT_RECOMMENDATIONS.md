### 10. **IMPLEMENT RETARGETING & ADS (Once You Have Budget)**

**Why:** 98% of visitors don't buy on first visit. Bring them back.

**Phase 1: Free Retargeting (Month 1-3)**
- Facebook Pixel installed ✓
- Build audience of 1,000+ visitors
- Create lookalike audiences
- Don't spend yet - just collect data

**Phase 2: Low-Budget Testing (Month 3-6)**
**Budget: $10/day = $300/month**

**Platform Priority:**
1. **YouTube Ads** (Best ROI for you)
   - Target: "book of enoch", "dragon mythology", "gnostic texts"
   - Video ad: 30-second teaser
   - Goal: 100,000 impressions
   - Expected: 2,000 clicks @ $0.15 CPC
   - Conversion: 100 signups = $3 per signup

2. **Reddit Ads**
   - Target: r/mythology, r/fantasy, r/worldbuilding
   - Cost: $5 CPC (expensive but targeted)
   - Better: Organic posting (free)

3. **Facebook/Instagram** (Last priority)
   - Expensive ($2-5 CPC)
   - But great for retargeting existing visitors
   - "Hey, you started reading..."

**Phase 3: Scaling (Month 6+)**
- Profitable ads? Scale to $50/day
- Unprofitable? Pause and optimize
- Focus on best-performing platform

**Impact:** With $300/month budget → 100-200 new users → 10-20 paying = $100-200/month profit
