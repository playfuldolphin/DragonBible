## 🎙️ **Recording Tips**

### Equipment:
- **Microphone:** Blue Yeti ($100) or Rode NT-USB ($169)
- **Software:** Audacity (free) or Adobe Audition
- **Music:** Epidemic Sound ($15/month) or Artlist ($199/year)
- **Video:** OBS Studio (free) or Camtasia

### Voice Tips:
- Record in closet (dampens echo)
- Speak 20% slower than normal
- Pause for 2 seconds between sentences
- Vary your tone (monotone = sleep inducing)
- Record multiple takes, use best

### Editing:
- Add reverb on key phrases
- Fade in/out music
- Normalize audio to -3dB
- Add captions (80% watch without sound)
