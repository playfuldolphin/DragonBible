Before you begin

A few things to know.

What is the Dragon Bible?

An original work of fantasy fiction that reimagines familiar sacred narratives through dragon mythology. It draws inspiration from Hermetic and Gnostic ideas; it is not religious guidance or a claim about historical events.

How much is available to read?

27 chapters across seven books are currently published. The library displays the actual chapter count for each book. More is being written.

Do I need an account?

No. The published lore is free. Reading progress and bookmarks are saved in this browser, so they do not automatically follow you to another device. Clearing browser storage removes them.

How does narration work?

The reader uses your browser’s text-to-speech voices. Voice availability varies by device; some voices need an internet connection. Choose a voice and speed in the reader.

Where should I start as a DM?

Read the free sampler, then the first-session guide. The setting is still developing; the free materials show what is available today.
