Privacy Policy

Service descriptions updated: October 6, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to The Dragon Bible ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website dragonbible.com (the "Site"). Please read this privacy policy carefully.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Reading Notes: Notes and earlier comments stay in your browser. They are not published or sent to our server.
  • Oracle Questions: When live AI is connected, questions are sent to our server and Anthropic to generate a response. Prewritten examples run in your browser.
  • Payments: When checkout is connected, Stripe processes your payment. The Site receives purchase and subscription status; card details are entered on Stripe.
  • Contact Information: If you contact us directly, we may receive additional information about you.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Hosting: Requests to the Site include connection information such as your IP address and browser headers. Our hosting provider may keep request logs.
  • Oracle Limits: When live AI is connected, the server temporarily tracks free question usage by IP address for a 24-hour limit.
  • Local Storage: Reading progress, bookmarks, notes, and a verified purchase access token are saved in your browser. The mobile quest prototype also saves your dragon's name, appearance, completed quests, and days played locally. These records do not automatically sync to another device.

3. Third-Party Services

3.1 Optional Payments and Live AI

Stripe and Anthropic are used only when their respective services are connected. The Site displays whether checkout and live AI are available. Their handling of information is described in Stripe's privacy policy and Anthropic's privacy policy.

3.2 Google Fonts

Our Site uses Google Fonts. When you visit our Site, your browser downloads the required fonts directly from Google servers. This may involve transmitting your IP address to Google.

4. Use of Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain our Site
  • Verify purchases and provide access to connected services
  • Improve user experience
  • Apply free Oracle usage limits
  • Respond to inquiries

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

This version of the Site does not load advertising or analytics scripts. Browser local storage supports the reader and verified paid access. External services such as Stripe may use their own cookies when you visit them.

You can clear Site data through your browser settings. Doing so removes saved progress, bookmarks, notes, and access tokens from that browser.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Browser Controls: Manage or clear saved Site data using your browser settings.
  • Do Not Track: We currently do not respond to Do Not Track signals

7. Data Retention

Reading progress, bookmarks, notes, and earlier comments remain in browser storage until you clear Site data. We do not store these reading records on our server. The live Oracle's free usage counter expires after 24 hours. Contact and payment records are separate from browser data.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Site is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

10. Third-Party Links

Our Site may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites.

11. International Users

If you access our Site from outside the United States, please be aware that your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

14. California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you have specific rights regarding your personal information under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

15. GDPR Rights (For EU Residents)

If you are a resident of the European Union, you have certain rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to access, rectify, or erase your personal data.
