Before the collection opens

When can I order?

The collection is being prepared for launch. Orders are not open yet. This page will link to checkout once the store is ready.

Where will orders ship?

The planned launch uses Printful Quick Stores, which currently delivers only to US addresses. Shipping charges and applicable tax will be shown at checkout.

How will the pieces be made?

Each piece is designed for on-demand production through Printful. The collection uses printed artwork; the gold appearance is not metallic foil. Product images are digital previews, and physical samples have not yet been inspected.

Does the merch unlock the app or books?

The published library and The Remembering prototype are free to explore. These are physical items; they do not include a subscription or paid app benefits.
